Confidence states: `VERIFIED` | `LIKELY` | `COUNSEL_REVIEW` | `VENDOR_CONFIRMATION` | `UNRESOLVED`.
Full citations and source URLs are in the two validation-pass documents from the design conversation; this file carries only what implementation needs plus enough citation to re-find the source.

**Standing rule:** any new legal/compliance claim discovered during implementation gets a row here with a confidence level before it is wired into behavior. Never convert an `UNRESOLVED` or `LIKELY` row into hard-coded logic without moving it to `VERIFIED` or `COUNSEL_REVIEW` first.
