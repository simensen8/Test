Step 1 · Roster

From a Weekly Attendance workbook (.xlsx or .ods). It carries every participant's name and PCC ID, which is what makes it the complete roster. The week you choose is also loaded as attendance, so pick a representative recent week.

Step 2 · Payer rules

From the consolidated Rate Master (.xlsx). Only participants with a payer exception appear on it; everyone else defaults to Private Pay. What it uniquely carries is the rotation rules, so this step is what lets reconciliation know that someone bills three days private pay and then a day on the grant.

Rules from a previous import are superseded. Anything typed onto a profile by hand is kept.
